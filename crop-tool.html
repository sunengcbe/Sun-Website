Gallery Photo Crop Tool

  1. Choose a photo (or drop it on the box). JPG, PNG and iPhone HEIC photos all work; HEIC is turned into JPG automatically.
  2. Drag to move it and use the zoom slider (or mouse wheel / pinch) until it looks right.
  3. Download (saved small, under 600 KB, so it uploads fast), then upload the downloaded file in Pages CMS → Gallery Page.
