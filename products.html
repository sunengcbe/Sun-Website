Our Products

We manufacture a comprehensive range of engineered threaded and knurled components designed to meet diverse industrial requirements.

01

Threaded Bars

We provide fully threaded bars and rods focused on strength, smooth finish, and consistent form. Available to your specifications with cutting and chamfering options.

02

Studs

Specialized in the production of threaded studs for both custom and standard specifications and sizes. We offer custom-made double ended studs, single ended studs, and fully threaded studs in Unified, Metric, and other thread forms and capable of rolling with a hardness of approximately RC 42.

03

Bolts

We manufacture high-quality threaded bolts, offering a versatile range tailored to both custom and standard specifications in a variety of sizes.

04

Knurled Components

We specialize in manufacturing knurled components designed for enhanced grip and reliable performance. Profiles include Diamond, Straight, and Square, based on requirements.

05

  • Grinding

  • Heat Treating
  • Plating
